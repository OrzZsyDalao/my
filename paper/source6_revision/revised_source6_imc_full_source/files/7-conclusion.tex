\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

CLASP compares network-transition and feasible-corridor distributions over the
same traceroute segments. In the existing one-hour RIPE Atlas snapshot,
projection-score Layer Agreement is 0.632 for 67 island and archipelagic
service-facing units and 0.001 for 202 coastal-mainland units; the geographic
ordering remains under equal-share and Top-1 allocation. The service-facing
population is not uniformly contractive: 81 of 275 units are network-broad/
corridor-concentrated, while 158 remain broad at both layers. Network-layer
diversity therefore does not provide one geography-independent estimate of
feasible physical-corridor diversity in the measured populations.
